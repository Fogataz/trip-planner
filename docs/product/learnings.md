# Learnings

> **Living register.** Started 2026-11-13 (sys-D5). What we learned, from what evidence, and what it changes.
>
> Until now a bet was settled and the lesson stayed in a chat or a retro note. This page is where it goes instead, so the next initiative starts from it. `/milestone`, `/audit` and research write-ups add rows. `/roadmap` and `/initiative` read them before recommending anything. A change to [strategy.md](strategy.md) or [working-doc.md](working-doc.md) at a gate cites the rows it rests on.

## How to write a row

- **One claim per row**, in a sentence a stranger could check: "Hosts set up a departure in under two hours once the agenda import works", not "import is good".
- **Evidence is a link**: an interview write-up in [research/](research/), a milestone verdict, an analytics count, a pull request. No link, no row.
- **Confidence:**
  - **strong** — measured, or three or more independent sources agree;
  - **medium** — two sources, or one measurement with caveats;
  - **weak** — one anecdote, or our own reading of it.
- **Changes** names what it should move: an initiative, an epic, a line of strategy, a spec, a way of working. "Nothing yet" is a valid answer.
- **Status:** open, acted on (with the link to what changed), or superseded by K*n*. Rows are never deleted.
- Codes are `K#` (knowledge) with a short name, `K3 · Hosts plan in Sheets`. `L#` is taken by the design lenses ([process/naming.md](../process/naming.md) §2).
