## How we run a test

1. **Interview first.** Ask about the last trip or event they ran, not about our idea: tools, time spent, money spent, what broke, what guests asked.
2. **Concierge pilot.** If there's a fit, we set up one real departure or event for them, by hand where needed. Cap the setup time (premium-buyer lens §6).
3. **Ask for money or a commitment.** Pre-sell the next one at a test price, or ask for a written intent.
4. **Record** the outcome in this register (status and next step), and the learnings in the working doc.

**Interview questions (operators and hosts):**

1. Walk me through your last trip, from first sign-up to the last day.
2. What tools did you use (forms, sheets, WhatsApp, payments)? What do they cost you?
3. What did travellers ask you most, before and during the trip?
4. What went wrong, or took the most time?
5. If one thing could disappear from your work, what would it be?
6. Would you pay to make that disappear? How much is a departure worth to you?
