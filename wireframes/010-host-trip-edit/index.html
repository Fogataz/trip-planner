Notes

Same three fields and the same length choice as trip creation, so one shared form component serves both (F8).

"Not decided" is new. It replaces the bare "TBD" with an explicit choice and lets the host clear a date they entered by mistake.

Origin and destination are not editable in this spec (see spec §6, D2).

Save is disabled until something changed. Errors show under the field: empty name, end before start (F6).

4. Dates change affects existing stops F7

Move the trip to 30 Nov – 2 Dec?
3 stops fall outside the new dates. They stay in the plan, and Plan will mark them "Outside trip dates".
BackChange dates
Notes

Shown only when at least one stop would fall outside the new range. Otherwise Save applies directly.

Recommendation: never delete or move stops automatically (spec D3). Stop times are exact instants; shifting them silently could hide a booked train.

If the change moves the trip to another phase (for example Planning to Countdown), nothing extra is asked; Overview just adapts.

7. Error and loading states

Saving: the button reads "Saving…" and is disabled. Failure: the panel stays open with the message under the form and nothing is lost. Offline: same as failure. Two organizers editing at once: last save wins, and both see the result live (spec D4).
