How to read these

Same conventions as the 002 set: greyscale, phone first at 390×844, grey block = image, dashed box = explanatory copy or a drop target.

Two kinds of annotation. Orange is the frame's requirements, as before. Blue marks a frame that fixes a QA finding (Q1–Q12 in spec 012 §4) and names what the current spec or frame does instead.

The rule these frames encode

Nothing administrative before the second person. The aha moment is the second person appearing in the trip, not the trip existing — so every question between first sign-in and that moment has to be one the product cannot work without.

And on attribution: an Organization is never the author of anything. People write, post and host; Organizations own and vouch.

1 · Attribution — who is this from?

Two questions, asked by two different readers. "Is this real?" is answered by the Organization. "Do I know who is asking me?" is answered by a person with a face. A link arriving cold in a WhatsApp group needs both; a personal trip needs neither.

2 · The guest's first five minutes

Ask for exactly what the next screen cannot work without, and nothing else. For the approval queue that is a name a host can recognise — and only when the provider did not give us one.

3 · The host's first trip — no Organization anywhere

Under O1 a person can sign up, create a hosted event, publish an agenda, share the link and approve guests without ever meeting the word Organization. This is the frame that proves it.

4 · The Organization, when it finally earns its keep

Offered at the first moment it does something the user wants — continuity — and never before. These three frames are Phase 1; the pilot's Organization is created by hand.

5 · Three doors — and the one the pilot actually uses

There is not one way in. There are three, and they differ by who has already decided about you. Door A is someone a host added by name: the decision is made, so the product acts on it instead of asking again. For the pilot's 38 attendees this is the dominant path, and spec 002 routes all of them through request-and-wait.

What these thirteen frames do not cover

The Organization switcher and the trip list stay as drawn in 002 frame 13, minus the permanent "Create an Organization" row (Q6). Join requests, the invite link panel, agenda import, draft review, the event home, announcements, notification settings and the language switcher are all in the 002 set and unchanged by this spec.

Out of scope here and not drawn: the Organization home page beyond this member list, billing and payouts, interests onboarding, avatars beyond what Google gives and what frame 4 offers (spec 007 owns profiles), moving a trip back out of an Organization, and the naming decision behind O2 — whether "Organization" is even the right word, which belongs with brand foundations §3.
