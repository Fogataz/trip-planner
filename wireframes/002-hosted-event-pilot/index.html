Hosted event pilot — lo-fi wireframes

Spec 002: docs/specs/002-hosted-event-pilot.md (Status: draft). The 13 frames proposed in §6 of that spec.

Everything on this page is static markup. Nothing is interactive, and none of it is production code.

How to read these

Lo-fi on purpose: greyscale, no brand colour, no type or icon decisions. Judge structure only — what sits where, what is loudest, and what is deliberately absent.

Grey block = image. Dashed box = a drop target or an empty state, not a reserved feature.

F / N / AC / S numbers refer to spec 002. Every frame carries the requirements it has to satisfy.

Standing content

The sample event throughout is the pilot: eDreams ODIGEO Gathering, 24–25 Nov 2026, Madrid, hosted by Alberto C., 38 attendees.

Times show in Madrid time and are labelled, whatever the viewer's device zone (F35, AC 28). Phone first at 390×844; desktop is the same layout, wider. The one layout that genuinely differs on desktop is frame 8, called out in its annotation.

Open decisions drawn as defaults

S1 join page shows name, dates, host, cover — frame 1.
S2 paste plus .md / .txt / .csv — frame 7.
S6 manifest and in-app only on iPhone — frames 4 and 10.
S7 anyone may create an Organization, not promoted — frame 13.
S8 announcements are one-way — frames 9 and 10.

These are the §9 recommendations drawn as the working default (D27), not owner decisions. Overriding one changes the frame it is listed against.

1 · The door — link to "request sent", under 60 seconds

Four frames, one flow. Nothing about the event beyond name, dates, host and cover is reachable before a host approves you (principle 3, F12, F15). The whole row is reachable logged out and in the visitor's detected language (F33, AC 26).

2 · Host — letting people in

One active link per trip, and an approval queue that survives 38 people arriving in the same five minutes after the link lands in a WhatsApp group.

3 · Host — the agenda as a draft

Principle 4 in two frames: AI output is a draft a human publishes. Nothing in these screens touches the schedule until the host taps Publish (F21, F22, AC 15).

4 · Announcements — one channel, not noise

One-way (S8), pinned at the top of the event, with a home in the app and not only in push (benchmark: Whova, Luma). Questions go to the existing Q&A.

5 · Settings — notifications, language, organization

Three small screens that carry three of the spec's load-bearing promises: attendees own their notifications, each person gets their own language, and Organizations exist without taking over the product.

What these frames do not cover

Spec 002 §10 is out of scope and not drawn: RSVP and check-in, an Organization home page beyond this switcher and a member list, public event pages, payments, interests onboarding, an activity feed, chat, Apple sign-in, announcements by email (S6), machine translation.

Three screens the spec needs but §6 did not list — create an Organization and the Organization member list (F1, F2 — the member list is named in §10 as in scope), and the trip people and roles screen where a host changes someone between host, co-host and guest (F4) — were drawn on 2026-11-11 in the 012 set, frames 6, 8 and 9. They live there because what they show is decided by spec 012, not by this spec.
